# Clinic video templates: Meta-safe format

Vertical ad and Reel template for the dental clinics (T.LOW, I-SIHAT and others). It's the format approved in Nov 2026 after Meta's own buttons covered the old bottom banner.

## Why "Meta-safe"
On Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories, Meta draws its own UI on top of the video:
- **Top ~14% (y < 270):** header, profile name, "Sponsored"
- **Bottom ~35% (y > 1250):** "Chat on WhatsApp" / CTA button, ad text, page name
- **Right edge (~150px, lower half):** like / comment / share icons

The old template put the price banner in the bottom 500px, so the CTA button covered it. **All text and graphics now sit inside y 262–1250, x 70–930.**

## Layout rules (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC 48k)
| Element | Position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–2.5s) | logo pill at y≈242, two boxed lines below it (white, then brand accent) | Keep the boxes off the face. For a close talking head, put them over the chest (top ≈ 915). Captions are hidden while the hook shows. |
| **Banner** | compact rounded card, ~1000x250–300, top edge at **y=262** | Slides down and fades in after the hook, then stays until the end card. Shows logo, headline, claim/price box, and optional photos and WhatsApp. |
| Key-point pills / chips | just under the banner, y≈520–600 | Numbered reasons, METAL/CERAMIC labels, etc. |
| Captions | bottom edge at **y=1240** (one position for all shots) | Inter ExtraBold 68–72, white with a dark outline, active word in the brand accent, 3–4 words per line. On a wide shot where people sit low, they can move up under the banner (y≈600). |
| B-roll cards (close-up / compare) | between y≈590 and y≈1150 | Card width ≤ 620 (single) or 2×420 (compare). |
| End card (3.5–4s) | everything between y≈285 and y≈1240 | Logo card, 2-line CTA, claim/price, accent "WhatsApp to book" button with the number, website/location, "Follow for more dental tips". |
| Bottom 35% | **no text** | The footage just runs underneath. Meta covers it anyway. |

## Smooth graphics (no jagged edges)
- Draw every rounded shape (banner card, buttons, hook boxes, photo frames) at **3x size, then downscale with LANCZOS**. Shapes drawn at 1x come out jagged.
- Banner bottom accent: a two-layer soft wave (a faint layer plus a solid layer) clipped to the card's rounded mask, so it follows the corners.
- Add a thin white outline (alpha ~170) around the card so it reads cleanly over busy footage.
- Logo cut-out: slightly blur and boost the alpha edge, so the edge isn't crunchy.

## Files
```
ffmpeg/                     ffmpeg + Pillow pipeline (no Node needed)
  tlow_build_metasafe.py    T.LOW "First Braces Appointment": B-roll + separate voiceover, top banner with before/after
  isihat_build_metasafe.py  I-SIHAT Invisalign: talking-head jump cuts, numbered key-point pills, perks banner
  music.py                  original generated music + whoosh/pop SFX (MUSIC_DUR=<seconds>)
  transcribe_clips.py       faster-whisper word timestamps for every clip -> work/transcripts.json
remotion/                   Remotion 4 project (talking head + B-roll cards)
  src/Main.tsx              per-video config block at the top (CLIPS, HOOK, CHIPS, ZOOMS, BROLL, END)
  src/theme.ts              colours + Meta-safe constants (SAFE, BANNER_TOP, BELOW_BANNER, CAPTION_BOTTOM)
  prep_metasafe.py          KEEP ranges -> captions.json, cleaned voice (fixed gain to -14 LUFS), B-roll, cards, fonts
  music_clinic.py           original "clinic" music + SFX timings (MUSIC_DUR=<seconds>)
```
The banner image used by Remotion (`public/banner.png`) is generated by the ffmpeg builder's banner section. Run that part first, or reuse an existing banner.png.

## Workflow for a new video
1. Download the footage and brand assets from Drive into `raw/` and `brand/`.
2. Transcribe locally (faster-whisper; `small` for English, `large-v3` for Malay). Fix misheard words in the `fix` map. Keep Malay as spoken.
3. Plan the shots and story order, and tighten pauses with jump cuts that alternate between normal and 1.12x punch-in.
4. Copy the closest builder, then edit the per-video block: cuts/segments, hook text, banner headline/claim/price, pills, phone number, end card text and the output name.
5. Run `MUSIC_DUR=<length+1> python3 music.py`, then the builder. To re-render overlays only, use `SKIP_VIDEO=1 python3 <builder>.py`.
6. **Check:** grab frames at the hook, the banner, a B-roll shot and the end card, and tint y<270 and y>1250 red. Nothing important may sit in the red. Zoom into the banner to check its edges. Loudness should be about −14 LUFS (`ffmpeg -i out.mp4 -af ebur128 -f null -`).
7. Deliver the MP4, listing the transcript fixes, the shot plan and anything unsure (prices, phone numbers, unclear words).

### Remotion render
```
cd remotion && npm i
npx remotion render src/index.ts Main ../work/video.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=17 --muted --browser-executable=<chrome-headless-shell>
```
Then mix the voice, music and SFX with ffmpeg (sidechain-duck the music at volume 0.17, then use alimiter). Adjust the voice gain until the mix is about −14 LUFS.
